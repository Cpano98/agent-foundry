- How to ask for the check

### Social norms
- Punctuality: strict, flexible, or "island time"
- Queueing: respected or informal
- Volume in public spaces (loud or quiet culture)
- Bargaining: expected, acceptable, or offensive
- Gestures to avoid (thumbs up, pointing, OK sign — vary by country)

### Photography rules
- Temples / religious sites: allowed / restricted areas
- Taking photos of people: ask first? never?
- Military / police / government buildings: always forbidden
- Markets and food stalls: usually okay, but ask

- Prayer times that affect schedules (Friday, Sunday, Ramadan…)
- Sacred items / clothing in temples / mosques / churches
- Shoes on or off (when to check)
