## 🗾 Japan

### Logistics
- **Suica card**: buy at airport arrival (penguin icon machines), ¥500 deposit + load ¥3,000. Works on all
  metro, JR, buses and convenience stores. Refund the deposit at any JR machine before leaving.
- **JR Pass**: almost never worth it for Tokyo-Kyoto-Osaka. Calculate individual tickets — they're cheaper.
- **Shinkansen**: book Nozomi/Hikari in advance (especially Green Car). Window seat D = Mt. Fuji view
  (~40 min after Tokyo departure, between km 170-200).
- **Coin lockers**: at every JR station, ¥400-700/bag/day. App "Japan Locker" for availability.
- **Tax-free**: from ¥5,000 per purchase, show passport at checkout. ~10% back.

### Timing (critical)
- **Fushimi Inari**: 7:00am max. At 9am tour buses arrive. The difference is absolute.
- **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove**: 8:00am. Same reason.
- **Senso-ji**: before 9am for the temple itself; Nakamise market opens ~10am anyway.
- **Nara deer park**: before 9am for calm; deer are fed by staff at 8am.
- **TeamLab**: 8am slot is ¥830 cheaper than 9:30+ slots and has no crowds.
- **Shibuya Sky**: opens reservations ~4 weeks before. Slot 16:30 = sunset at 17:10. Sells out fast.

### Apps
- Google Maps (shows exact platform), Google Translate (camera on menus), Hyperdia (metro routing), PayPay
  (QR payments), USJ App (Nintendo World timed entry).

### Food by city
**Tokyo:** Tsukiji for seafood breakfast, ramen in Akihabara back streets, izakaya in Golden Gai (Shinjuku)
**Kyoto:** tofu kaiseki near Kiyomizudera, Pontocho canal dinner, Nishiki Market for snacks
**Osaka:** takoyaki Wanaka or Kukuru, kushikatsu in Shinsekai (never double-dip the sauce), kaiten-zushi,
  Kuromon Market for fresh seafood bites. Osaka has the highest restaurant density per m² in the world.
**Nara:** kakinoha-zushi (sushi wrapped in persimmon leaf) — local specialty found nowhere else.

### Attractions to book in advance
| Attraction | Lead time | Notes |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Shibuya Sky | ~4 weeks | Sunset slots (16:00-17:00) sell out first |
| TeamLab Planets | 2-4 weeks | Book 8am for price + no crowds |
| Tokyo Skytree | 1-2 weeks | Combo 350m+450m is the right ticket |
| USJ ExpressPass | 2-4 weeks | Required for Nintendo World + HP without 2h queues |
| Shinkansen Green Car | 1+ month | Especially October peak season |

### Nara day trip from Osaka
Kintetsu Namba → Kintetsu Nara: Express, ~45 min, ¥680. Depart at 8:00, arrive 8:45.
Sequence: Nara Park deer → Todai-ji (¥1,000) → Kasuga Taisha → lunch (kakinoha-zushi) → return 15:30.

### Budget reference (October 2026, 3 travelers)
- Food per person per day: ¥7,000-11,000 (mid-range + antojitos + desserts)
- Local transport per person per day: ¥500-1,700
- Entries per day: ¥0-3,500 (varies widely)
- Shopping intense per person (full trip): ¥160,000-170,000
- **Recommended total per person on the ground (13 days): ¥290,000 ≈ $1,930 USD ≈ $33,750 MXN**
- Exchange rate reference: ¥150 = $1 USD = $17.50 MXN (verify before travel)
